About Us

We're on a Mission to Make AI Honest

Aivora AI was built because the AI tool review space is broken — full of paid rankings, sponsored posts, and fake verdicts. We're changing that.

Our Story

Born from Frustration

In 2024, Varun Lalwani was tired of being misled. Every AI tool review he read was glowing. Every comparison chart had the same winner. It was obvious — most review sites were being paid to praise tools they barely tested.

So he started Aivora AI with one rule: tell the truth, always. Every tool gets tested hands-on. Every review includes what's bad, not just what's good. And no tool can buy its way to a good rating.

What started as a personal project quickly became one of the most trusted AI review destinations, now reaching 50,000+ readers per month from over 80 countries.

Our Values

What We Stand For

🔬

Rigorous Testing

We use every tool in real workflows before forming an opinion. No spec-sheet reviews. No paraphrasing marketing copy.

🚫

Editorial Independence

No tool company pays us for a good review. Our ratings are based 100% on our own hands-on experience.

💬

Radical Honesty

We say when a tool is bad. We highlight limitations, pricing issues, and red flags — even if it loses us affiliate revenue.

🌍

Global Perspective

We review tools from a global lens — considering availability, pricing in different regions, and use cases worldwide.

🔄

Always Updated

AI tools change fast. We revisit and update our reviews regularly to ensure the advice you read is still accurate.

❤️

Reader First

Every piece of content is written for you — the reader trying to make a smart decision — not for the search engine.
