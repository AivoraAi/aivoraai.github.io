Common Questions

Do you accept paid reviews or sponsored posts? ▾
No. We do not accept payments for reviews or sponsored rankings. All reviews are based entirely on our own hands-on testing. We may use affiliate links (disclosed), but they never influence our ratings.
Can I suggest an AI tool for review? ▾
Absolutely! Use the contact form above and select "Request a Tool Review." We try to review all suggested tools, though we can't guarantee a timeline.
I found incorrect information — how do I report it? ▾
We take accuracy seriously. Use the form above and select "Correction/Update Request." We'll investigate and update any confirmed inaccuracies promptly.
Are you open to guest post collaborations? ▾
We consider high-quality guest contributions from genuine AI experts. Reach out with your topic idea and writing samples. We don't accept pure link-building content.
